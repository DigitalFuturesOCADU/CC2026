Experiment 2 · One Phone, More People

Eight examples. Motion, touch and sound go in. The screen, sound and the flashlight come out. Each one has a version for 1, 2 and 3 people sharing one phone. The output is the same in all three. Only the number of people changes, so try all three and compare. On a laptop, each sketch shows a QR code to open it on a phone.

  • 01 Tilt to DrawMotion → screen. In turn: the phone is passed every 15 seconds, and each person draws in their own colour.
    • 1 person codeTip the phone to steer the pen. One line, one colour.
    • 2 people codeDraw for 15 seconds, then pass the phone when the colour changes. Two colours build up on one drawing.
    • 3 people codeThe phone goes around three people, 15 seconds each. Three colours on one drawing.
  • 02 Tilt to SpeedMotion → sound. In turn: one recording loops, and each person gets their own copy of it, their own voice.
    • 1 person codeTilt to bend the speed of the loop. Flat is slow, upright is fast.
    • 2 people codeSet a speed and pass the phone. Your voice keeps playing while the second person bends a second voice against it.
    • 3 people codeThree voices at three speeds. When the phone comes back around, you change your own voice again.
  • 03 Still for LightMotion → flashlight. Together: the light comes on when the phone has been still for a second. The code is the same in all three.
    • 1 person codeRest the phone face down on your palm. Hold still and the light comes on.
    • 2 people codeTwo people hold the phone up together on their fingertips. It stays lit only while both are steady.
    • 3 people codeThree people on their fingertips. The light shows how still three people can be together.
  • 04 Hold to RevealTouch → screen. Side by side: one strip of the screen for each person. A finger on your strip shows your part of the picture.
    • 1 person codeHold a finger anywhere and the whole picture fades in.
    • 2 people codeOne half each. The whole picture shows only when both of you are touching.
    • 3 people codeOne strip each. Each person shows a third. The whole picture needs all three.
  • 05 Hold a NoteTouch → sound. Side by side: one strip for each person, one note for each strip. Slide up for higher, down for lower.
    • 1 person codeOne strip, one note. Hold to play it, slide to change it.
    • 2 people codeTwo notes. Each person moves their own, and you both hear the two together.
    • 3 people codeThree notes: a chord made by three people.
  • 06 Hands for LightTouch → flashlight. Together: the light blinks faster with each finger on the screen, and stays on when everyone is touching.
    • 1 person codeTouch the screen and the light turns on. Let go and it turns off.
    • 2 people codeOne finger makes a slow blink. It stays on only when both of you are touching.
    • 3 people codeOne finger: a slow blink. Two: faster. All three: steady on.
  • 07 Hum to PlaySound → screen. Together: the robot runs while there is sound and freezes in silence. The code is the same in all three.
    • 1 person codeHum and it runs. When you stop for breath, it freezes.
    • 2 people codeTwo people hum, overlapping their breaths so it keeps running.
    • 3 people codeThree people hum in a relay. With three, it never has to stop.
  • 08 Quiet for LightSound → flashlight. Together: the light comes on after three seconds of silence, and any sound turns it off. The code is the same in all three.
    • 1 person codeBe quiet for three seconds and the light comes on.
    • 2 people codeThe light needs both of you to be quiet.
    • 3 people codeThe light comes on only when all three of you agree to be silent.
